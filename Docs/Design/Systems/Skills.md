# Skills

**Status:** Concept

Skills represent the survivor's proficiency. They are opened from the nav bar or the S hotkey ([Controls](../UI/Controls.md)). How skills are gained and their levels are not yet designed.

## Skills identified so far

| Skill | Affects |
|---|---|
| Melee fighting | Hit chance with close melee weapons (knife, sword) ([Combat](Combat.md)) |
| Pole weapon fighting | Hit chance with reach weapons (spear, axe) |
| Ranged fighting | Hit chance with ranged weapons (stone, sling, bow) |
| First aid | Treating injuries ([Injuries and Health](Injuries.md#treatment)) |
| Swimming | Risk while swimming ([Exertion](Exertion.md#risk-tiers)) |
| Climbing | Risk while climbing |
