# Tutorial

**Status:** Concept - proposals from squeezy102's notes (2026-10-19), not approved ([#65](https://github.com/squeezy102/SqueezySurvival/issues/65))

The opening hours guide the player toward their first choices without forcing them.

## Waking up

The survivor wakes on the beach, filthy, hungry and thirsty, with no idea how they got there ([GDD: opening scenario](../GameDesignDocument.md#opening-scenario)).

The game suggests washing off first, then exploring, but never locks the player into either. The **ocean** is a permanent zone feature of the Beach, offering Wash, Fish and Dive.

## Scripted first finds

The first explores always turn up **a stick, a stone and a coconut**, the keys to getting started. Once the survivor has all three, the game explains how to use them. (Today's scripted finds also include extra stones, sticks, seaweed and a seashell ([Exploration: wireframe stage](../Systems/Exploration.md#wireframe-stage)); they change to fit.)

## First crafts

The game explains, one step at a time:

1. Holding one stone over another makes a **sharpened stone**, a rough cutting tool.
2. The cutting tool punches a hole in the coconut to drink its water.
3. The coconut can be cracked open, and its meat taken out with another action.
4. Husking it gives fiber; its empty shells hold liquids.
5. Fiber combined with fiber makes **simple cordage**.
6. Finding things opens new research: with a sharpened stone, a stick and cordage, **hand tools** (a knife and a hand axe) appear in the research window, and the game shows the player where.

## The interface

A short tour of the screen: hunger and thirst, sanity, clicking a meter for detail, the time and day phase, the zone, its features and ground, the inventory and hands, and the actions. Fleshed out as more of the game is built.

## Skipping

The tutorial can always be skipped: by unchecking **Tutorial** when starting a game (checked by default), or from the pause menu at any time during it.
