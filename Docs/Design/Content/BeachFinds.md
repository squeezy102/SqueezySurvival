# Beach Finds

**Status:** Concept - first pass from squeezy102's notes (2026-10-19), not approved. Needs a realism check: what a person could actually find on a tropical beach.

What the survivor can find lying around on the Beach, the first zone. Replaces the [placeholder content](../Systems/Exploration.md#wireframe-stage) once approved.

## Finds

| Find | Uses and notes |
|---|---|
| Stone | Small, ordinary. Knapping, campfires, crafting. Holding one stone over another makes a sharpened stone at the cost of one or both; it may break. |
| Big rock | Large and heavy. Knaps tools without breaking (a stone held over it becomes a sharpened stone at the cost of the stone); deadfall traps; a counterweight. |
| Small stick | Thumb-thick branches. Kindling, hand tools, fuel, traps. Carves into wood shavings. |
| Big stick | Wrist-thick limbs. Heavier tools, building, firewood. Chops into small sticks and shavings. |
| Log | Part of a trunk. Rare loose; usually from trees. Chops into big sticks and shavings. Building. |
| Seaweed | Edible as a green. Basic cordage; crude foot coverings, clothes and hats. |
| Coconut | Food and water, but too much causes diarrhea: an emergency source, not a sustainable one. Oil; fibrous husk (needs a sharp tool); shells become bowls. Also grows on palms. |
| Palm frond | A simple bed or shelter, baskets and packs, a crude shield, cordage, cooking. Burns, but is better used elsewhere. |
| Seashell | Crushes into a calcium-rich powder; jewelry and decorations lift morale. |
| Crab | Food, better cooked; a negligible chance of food poisoning raw. Shell crushes. Spoils fast once picked up unless cooked. |
| Lobster | Rarer. Food, cooked; a moderate chance of food poisoning raw. Shell crushes. Lobster traps catch more. Spoils fast. |
| Conch, clam, oyster | May hold an edible creature; clams and oysters rarely a pearl. Crush. Keep a few days unopened; spoil fast once opened. |
| Gooseneck barnacle | A very rare delicacy that makes the survivor very happy. Needs a sharp tool. |
| Coral | Dried; crushes. Little else. |
| Driftwood | Splits into a random mix of big and small sticks, or becomes a crude shield. Too crooked to build with. |
| Bone | Bone meal for fertilizer; bone tools (a knife, a spear). |
| Feather | Pillows, jackets, blankets, feather meal, arrows, darts, decorations, kindling. Also from birds and nests. |
| Cone snail | Best left alone ([Exploration: light and visibility](../Systems/Exploration.md#light-and-visibility)). Finding one logs a warning ("Found a cone snail. I've heard these are very, very dangerous. Best to leave it alone."); picking one up bare-handed carries a tiny chance of a sting. Beautiful shell; the creature inside is edible. |
| Hermit crab | Skittish: catching one is an encounter, and it usually gets away. Tasty cooked; shell crushes. |
| Sea sponge | Uncommon, dry and brittle. Soaks up liquids; helps bathing and cleaning wounds. More by diving. |
| Sand dollar | Inedible. Crushes into a calcium-rich powder. |
| Flotsam | A washed-up bundle that always holds one or more of the items below. |

### Flotsam

| Item | Uses and notes |
|---|---|
| Sea glass | Decoration and jewelry; a lens to start a fire, only in the brightest hours of a clear day. |
| Tangled net | A scrap of fishing net: cordage, or with tailoring skill, many patched into a usable net. |
| Tangled line | Cordage, with a very small chance of a usable line and hook. |
| Plastic bottle | Very rare. Carries liquids, never decays, and nothing evaporates from it. |
| Sea timber | Rotten lumber. Firewood only; a very small chance of 1 or 2 nails, which take effort to remove. |
| Rotten fish carcass | Bones, rarely a fish hook; compost. Handling it is gross and adds filth. |
| Rotten bird carcass | Bones, rarely feathers; compost. Gross; adds filth. |
| Message in a bottle | Extremely rare. Holds a message and a bottle that stops evaporation. Could carry messages from contributors and backers, or start a quest: [OQ-082](../OpenQuestions.md). |
