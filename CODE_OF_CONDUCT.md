# Code of Conduct

SqueezySurvival is a small project built by friends, open to anyone who wants to help. These rules apply everywhere the project lives: Discussions, issues, pull requests and reviews.

## Be good to each other

- **Be kind and respectful.** Disagree with ideas, never with people. No insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- **Be welcoming.** Newcomers and beginners are welcome; help them find their way.
- **Assume good intent.** Ask before assuming someone meant harm.
- **Keep it on topic.** Discussions are about the game and the project. Keep posts safe for everyone: no explicit, hateful or illegal content.
- **Credit others.** Only share work you have the right to share, and credit its source ([Asset Credits](Docs/Engineering/AssetCredits.md)).

## How decisions are made

- Everyone can suggest anything. squeezy102 has the final say on what goes into the game.
- A decision counts only once it is in the design docs, approved through a pull request ([Work Tracking](Docs/Engineering/WorkTracking.md#from-idea-to-work)).
- A "no" or "not now" is about the idea, not you.

## If something goes wrong

Contact squeezy102 directly on GitHub. Posts or comments that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed, and repeated or serious problems can lead to removal from the project.
